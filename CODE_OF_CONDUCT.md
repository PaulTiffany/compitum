# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the Contributor Covenant (v2.1). We expect all participants to be respectful and constructive.

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment‑free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behavior to: paulctiffany@gmail.com

Maintainers will review, investigate, and take appropriate action. For severe or unresolved issues, we may restrict participation to protect the community.
